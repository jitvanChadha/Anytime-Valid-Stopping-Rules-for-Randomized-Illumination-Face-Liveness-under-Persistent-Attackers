> Simulations (E1-E7) are fully synthetic. The per-round lag-noise parameter sigma was calibrated from timing jitter in live-subject videos of FRAUD1 [Smith et al., 2015], used under its non-commercial research licence (accessed 2026-10-14; SHA-256 eada19636a52087b57cb6652d182ee877741dfa62238f3535ba90b138096eeb8; 276 videos used, 24 rejected by edge-count QC; sigma_hat = 11.8 ms, 95% bootstrap CI 11.0-12.5 ms). Flash onsets were located with the 'peaks' detector and ROI mode 'auto'. A first-pass global-threshold detector rejected 267/300 videos and was replaced after inspecting the data by a per-flash adaptive detector (24/300 rejected). sigma_hat ranged 11.3-12.4 ms across detector/ROI configurations; ROI mode changed the rejection rate by 0.7 percentage points. FRAUD1 contains no injection attacks and is not used for any attack-success estimate.
